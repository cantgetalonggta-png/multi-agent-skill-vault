# Quality Gates — Music Video Clips

Reject or regenerate a clip if any of these are true:

- Face, age, hair, or costume drifted from the approved sheet
- Extra or missing fingers, collapsing wrists, sliding feet, rubber spine
- Mouth motion fights the lyric on a designated lip-sync shot
- Model music is audible under or instead of the master track
- Lighting direction or palette jumps versus adjacent shots
- Clip is longer than the model can hold and identity melts after ~6s
- Cut ignores the bar line when a downbeat cut was available
- Resolution, aspect, or fps does not match the delivery plan

Pass conditions for a release cut:

- Same person walks through the whole video
- Motion has weight
- Audio is the approved master, loudness-checked
- 16:9 and 9:16 exist if the release needs both
- No unlicensed real-artist clone
