# AI Video Production Pipeline

## Overview

Orchestrate professional AI-assisted video production from brief or script through final delivery. Combines AI video/avatar/image generation, voice synthesis, and traditional editing tools into a repeatable pipeline suitable for social, marketing, cinematic, and business content.

## Core Production Stages

1. Intake and Concept
   - Review client brief, script, references, target audience, platform, and visual style.
   - Clarify length, aspect ratio, brand constraints, and must-have elements.

2. Creative Direction
   - Define scenes, characters or avatars, environments, camera language, pacing, and overall tone.
   - Produce a lightweight shot list or storyboard notes when helpful.

3. Asset Generation
   - AI video clips (text-to-video or image-to-video)
   - AI avatars and presenters
   - Supporting stills and style frames
   - Voiceover and narration

4. Assembly and Editing
   - Timeline assembly in a professional NLE
   - Sync audio, color, transitions, text, graphics
   - Music bed and sound design

5. Post-Production Polish
   - Upscaling, stabilization, artifact cleanup when needed
   - Final color grade and audio mix
   - Platform-specific formatting

6. Delivery
   - Export correct resolution, frame rate, codec, and loudness
   - Provide versions if required (horizontal, vertical, square)
   - Deliver with project notes

## Quality Gates

- Visual consistency across scenes (character, lighting, style)
- Audio clarity and sync
- Platform-native framing and safe zones
- No obvious generation artifacts in final cut (or intentional stylistic use)
- Correct technical specs for the delivery platform

## Confirmation Points

Ask before:
- Long or expensive generation runs
- Switching major tools mid-project
- Final export that overwrites previous masters
- Any credential or paid-API usage
