# Shot Templates

## Lip-sync verse (camera lock)
A [age] [look] artist in [wardrobe], standing at a [mic / no mic] in [location]. Faces camera. Sings with [emotion]. Subtle rhythmic weight shift. Hands stay natural. No generated music. Cinematic lighting, [lens], locked camera.

## Walk-up chorus
Starts three-quarter, two measured steps toward camera, shoulders relaxed, head turns to lens on the last step, stops in a stable pose. Same wardrobe and face as refs. 35mm, slow dolly.

## Intimate close
Chest-up, shallow depth of field, motivated practical light. Micro-expressions, natural blink, no head whip. Hold for the lyric phrase.

## Dance burst
Full body, floor and headroom visible. [specific step]. Graceful arc on the arms. Lands in a balanced stance before the clip ends.

## B-roll / world
Empty or near-empty [location] at [time of day], camera [move], no identity required. Use to cover bars the performer cannot hold.

## 808 / drop energy
Wider frame, harder camera (push or whip only if the model can hold it), performer hits a still pose on the downbeat rather than flailing.
