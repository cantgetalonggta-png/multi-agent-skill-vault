# Example Prompts and Patterns

## Full Songs with Vocals
- "Make a hip-hop track about running SEO audits, English rap, energetic, 90 seconds"
- "Create a Latin pop / reggaeton song in Spanish about summer nights, upbeat, 2 minutes"
- "Japanese lo-fi / city pop song about late-night coding, soft male vocals, 75 seconds"
- "Jazz crooner with piano trio, lyrics about finding the first page of search results, relaxed swing"

## Instrumentals
- "Chill lo-fi beat, 60 seconds, rainy window mood, no vocals"
- "Epic orchestral trailer cue, 45 seconds, building intensity, cinematic"
- "Dark ambient electronic, 3 minutes, suitable for focus work"

## Covers and Style Transfer
- "Make a heavy rock cover of the previous track"
- "Turn this melody into a bossa nova arrangement"
- "Remake the chorus in a synthwave style"

## Section Edits (Repaint)
- "Make the chorus more energetic and louder"
- "Replace the bridge with a quiet piano interlude"
- "Extend the intro by 8 bars of atmospheric pads"

## Export Requests
- "Export the last track for Spotify"
- "Prepare a TikTok version, 15–30 seconds, loud and punchy"
- "Normalize for YouTube and keep full dynamics"

## Surprise / Random
- "Surprise me with something upbeat"
- "Random genre, about 90 seconds, instrumental"

## Good Prompt Structure
1. Core request (song / instrumental / cover)
2. Genre + sub-style
3. Subject / theme / lyrics direction
4. Vocal language and style (if any)
5. Length or energy
6. Optional constraints (BPM, key, instruments, mood)

Keep prompts concrete. Vague requests produce average results; specific musical language yields better tracks.
