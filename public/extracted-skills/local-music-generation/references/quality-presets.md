# Quality Presets

| Preset   | Approx. Time | Variants | Best For                          | Notes |
|----------|--------------|----------|-----------------------------------|-------|
| draft    | ~15 s        | 4        | Rapid ideation, style exploration | Lowest fidelity, good for iteration |
| standard | ~15 s        | 2        | Everyday production               | Balanced default |
| high     | ~25 s        | 1–2      | Better lyrics, structure, coherence | Prefer when vocals matter |
| max      | 3–5 min      | 1        | Highest quality possible          | Use only when quality is critical; confirm with user |

## Selection Guidance

- Start with `standard` for most requests.
- Drop to `draft` when the user is exploring many ideas or testing styles.
- Move to `high` or `max` only after the user confirms they want higher fidelity and accepts the time cost.
- Max preset is appropriate for final deliverables, client work, or when the user explicitly asks for the best possible quality.

## Parameter Influences

Higher presets generally improve:
- Lyric clarity and alignment
- Structural coherence (verse/chorus distinction)
- Instrumental detail and stereo imaging
- Overall musicality and fewer artifacts

They also increase VRAM pressure and generation time.
