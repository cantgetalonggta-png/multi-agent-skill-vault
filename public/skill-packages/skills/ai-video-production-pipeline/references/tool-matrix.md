# Tool Matrix for AI Video Production

Organize tools by role. Prefer the most reliable current option for each job; availability and quality change quickly.

## AI Video Generation
- Runway
- Kling AI
- Luma Dream Machine
- Google Veo / Flow
- Sora (when available)
- Pika Labs
- Seedance / Higgsfield (emerging)

## AI Avatars and Presenters
- HeyGen
- Synthesia
- Other talking-head / avatar platforms

## AI Image / Style Frames
- Midjourney
- FLUX
- Stable Diffusion XL variants
- Adobe Firefly
- Nano Banana / Magnific (upscale and enhance)

## Voice and Audio
- ElevenLabs (primary high-quality TTS and voice cloning)
- Platform-native voice tools when sufficient
- Music beds from local-music-generation skill or licensed libraries

## Editing and Post
- Adobe Premiere Pro
- DaVinci Resolve
- CapCut Pro (fast social workflows)
- Topaz AI / similar for upscaling and enhancement

## Typical Combinations
- Avatar talking-head + product B-roll: HeyGen or Synthesia + Runway/Kling B-roll + ElevenLabs + CapCut/Premiere
- Cinematic short: Kling/Runway/Luma primary generation + Midjourney style frames + Premiere/Resolve + music bed
- High-volume social: CapCut-centric with AI clip generation and auto-captions

Always verify current model capabilities and terms before production use.
