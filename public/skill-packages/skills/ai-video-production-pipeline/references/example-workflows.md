# Example End-to-End Workflows

## 1. Short Social Avatar Video (30–45 s)
1. Intake script and brand colors.
2. Generate avatar presenter in HeyGen/Synthesia with ElevenLabs voice.
3. Generate 2–4 B-roll clips in Runway or Kling matching the script beats.
4. Assemble in CapCut or Premiere: avatar on left or center, B-roll cutaways, captions, logo sting.
5. Add music bed from local-music-generation skill.
6. Export 9:16 1080p, loudness for TikTok/Reels.

## 2. Cinematic Storytelling Short (60–90 s)
1. Creative direction and shot list.
2. Generate key style frames in Midjourney/FLUX.
3. Generate video clips in Kling/Runway/Luma using style frames as reference.
4. Voiceover with ElevenLabs.
5. Full edit and grade in Premiere or Resolve.
6. Music and sound design.
7. Export 16:9 or cinematic aspect, high bitrate master + social cutdowns.

## 3. Marketing Product Video
1. Script focused on benefits + demo.
2. Mix of AI product visualization and real footage if available.
3. Avatar host or pure voiceover.
4. Fast-paced edit with text callouts.
5. Multiple aspect ratios for paid social and website.

Adapt the stages to the actual assets the user already possesses.
