# Common Platform Specs

## Aspect Ratios
- Vertical (Reels, Shorts, TikTok): 9:16
- Square: 1:1
- Horizontal / YouTube: 16:9
- Cinematic: 2.39:1 or 21:9 when intentional

## Typical Lengths
- Hook / ad (social): 6–15 s
- Short-form content: 15–60 s
- Mid-form: 1–3 min
- Long-form / documentary style: 3+ min

## Technical Defaults
- Frame rate: 24, 25, or 30 fps (match source or platform preference)
- Resolution: 1080x1920 (vertical), 1920x1080 (horizontal), 4K when source supports it
- Codec: H.264 or H.265 / ProRes for masters
- Audio: 48 kHz, stereo, normalized to platform loudness

## Safe Zones
Keep critical text and faces inside the central safe area for vertical video to avoid UI overlays.

Always re-check current platform recommendations before final delivery.
